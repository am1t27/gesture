How to use

Three camera modes, one workflow: keep your hand inside the green box, hold each shape steady, and watch the letter appear.

1

Set up your camera

Sit with a plain background behind your hand, and keep the hand and the wall clearly different in brightness. The threshold is chosen for you — check the mask panel shows a clean white hand on black. If it doesn't, switch that panel to manual and adjust.

2

Scan one letter

Hold a hand shape inside the green box. The letter appears once the shape holds still for a few frames, with a confidence figure beneath it. The box turns orange when no hand is detected.

3

Build a sentence

Sign a letter, then press C to add it to your sentence. Add a saved space gesture between words. When you're done, save the sentence — it's stored in your history and can be spoken aloud and exported from the desktop app.

4

Teach a custom gesture

Show the engine a new hand shape, give it a name, and save it. From then on it's recognized alongside the standard alphabet.
